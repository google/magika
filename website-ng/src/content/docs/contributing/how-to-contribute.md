---
title: How to Contribute
---

We would love to accept your patches and contributions to this project!

Check [GitHub's open issues labeled as "help wanted"](https://github.com/google/magika/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22help+wanted%22) as a starting point.

For our full contribution guidelines—including the Contributor License Agreement (CLA), code review process, and developer instructions such as updating the content types knowledge base—please see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/google/magika/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) on GitHub.
